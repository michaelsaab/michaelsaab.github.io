Project 02 · Personal

Car Audio Install

I installed an amplifier and a dual-subwoofer enclosure in my Jeep, running every connection myself: power, ground, signal, fuse, and remote turn-on.

TypePersonal project SystemAmplifier + dual subwoofers SkillsVehicle wiring, troubleshooting
Wiring

Five Connections, Each With a Job

An amplifier only works if every one of its connections is right, so I treated each one as its own piece of the system:

  • Power: a dedicated wire from the battery to feed the amplifier the current it needs.
  • Fuse: an inline fuse on that power wire, there to protect the wire itself if something shorts.
  • Ground: a solid connection to the vehicle's chassis, the return path for all that current.
  • Signal: the audio from the head unit that the amplifier boosts for the subwoofers.
  • Remote turn-on: a low-current wire that switches the amplifier on and off with the radio.
Behind the Dash

Getting to the Wiring

Reaching the head unit meant taking the dash apart, then routing wiring from the front of the vehicle back to the cargo area where the subwoofers sit.

Testing

Verifying It Works

Once everything was connected, I checked the system's operation and worked through the wiring issues that came up until it ran correctly. The biggest lesson: verify every connection before powering anything on.
