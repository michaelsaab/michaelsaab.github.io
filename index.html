MSU Solar Racing Team · Sensing & Data Acquisition

I'm a sophomore at Michigan State University double majoring in Electrical Engineering and Computer Engineering. I joined the MSU Solar Racing Team in September 2026 as a member of the Sensing & Data Acquisition team, where I design circuit boards in Altium for the electronics on our solar car.

I'm most interested in PCB design, embedded hardware, and embedded software. I got into electrical engineering because I enjoy designing hardware, and I like seeing a design go from a schematic to a layout to a physical board that has to work.

Right now I'm building my embedded skills by learning STM32 microcontrollers and C++, and getting more comfortable at the lab bench with oscilloscopes, function generators, and soldering.

Long term, I want to work on avionics and electronic systems in the defense industry.
